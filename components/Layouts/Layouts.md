1. **Single Column Layout**
  A simple, mobile-friendly layout where content is displayed in a single, vertical column.
2. **Multi-Column Layout**
  Uses multiple columns to display content side by side; common in newspapers, blogs, and dashboards.
3. **Grid Layout**
  Utilizes CSS Grid to create complex, responsive grids with rows and columns.
4. **Flexbox Layout**
  Employs CSS Flexbox for flexible and adaptive arrangements of items in rows or columns.
5. **Holy Grail Layout**
  Classic layout with header, footer, main content, and two sidebars (left/right).
6. **Sidebar Layout**
  Main content area with either a fixed or collapsible sidebar for navigation or tools.
7. **Card Layout (Masonry/Tile)**
  Content is distributed in cards or tiles, often with a masonry effect for dynamic height alignment.
8. **Split Screen Layout**
  Screen is divided vertically or horizontally into two (or more) resizable sections.
9. **Magazine/Editorial Layout**
  Visually rich layouts mimicking magazines with varying text and image areas, overlays, and feature sections.
10. **Dashboard Layout**
  Widget or card-based grid used for admin panels and analytic dashboards.
11. **Hero Layout**
  Prominent hero section at the top, often covering the full viewport width/height, followed by additional content regions.
12. **Z-Pattern Layout**
  Guides the user’s eye in a “Z” path; commonly used for landing pages and marketing sites.
13. **F-Pattern Layout**
  Content arranged to follow the reading pattern of users, focusing on top and left sides.
14. **Sticky/Fixed Header & Footer Layout**
  Headers or footers stay visible at the top/bottom of the viewport as users scroll.
15. **Off-canvas/Drawer Layout**
  Navigation or panels slide in from off the viewport, often for mobile menus or sidebars.
16. **Landing Page Layout**
  Focused, single-page layout for lead capture, sign-up, or sales conversion.
17. **Responsive/Adaptive Layout**
  Content adapts and reflows for different screen sizes and orientations using media queries.
18. **Tabbed Layout**
  Uses horizontal or vertical tabs to switch between content sections.
19. **Overlay/Layout with Modals**
  Employs overlays or modal dialogs to show additional content without navigation.
20. **Accordion Layout**
  Features collapsible sections for FAQs, menus, or dense information.
21. **Step or Wizard Layout**
  Guides users through a multistep process with sequential panels or cards.
